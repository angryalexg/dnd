Roleplay & Exploration Cheat Sheet
Always available — no cost
Cunning ActionBonus Action▶
Bonus action every turn: Dash, Disengage, or Hide. Use Disengage to escape melee without provoking opportunity attacks. Use Hide when you want Sneak Attack without a nearby ally.
Psychic BladesNo cost▶
Manifest a blade of psychic energy as part of your Attack action — no materials needed, can't be disarmed. After the primary attack, if your other hand is free you can make a bonus action attack with a second blade (1d4+4 psychic). Both blades are Finesse and Thrown (60/120 ft).
Halfling LuckPassive▶
When you roll a 1 on a d20 test (attack, save, ability check), reroll the die and use the new result. Happens automatically — no action needed.
Naturally StealthyPassive▶
You can attempt to Hide even when only obscured by a creature at least one size larger than you. Perfect for sneaking behind a large ally or creature.
Psionic abilities (cost psi dice)
Psi-Bolstered KnackMaybe costs 1 die▶
When you fail an ability check using a skill or tool you're proficient with, roll one Psionic Energy Die (d8) and add the result to the check. The die is only expended if the check now succeeds. No action required — just happens when you fail.
Psychic Whispers1 free / LR▶
Magic action: choose up to 3 creatures (= Proficiency Bonus), roll 1d8, establish a telepathic link for that many hours. All chosen creatures can communicate telepathically with each other. First use per Long Rest is free; additional uses cost 1 psi die each.
Key skills & tactics
Stealth +10 (Expertise)▶
Best skill. Passive Stealth 20. Use before combat for positioning. Naturally Stealthy lets you hide behind larger allies. Sneak Attack triggers when hidden (you have advantage).
Sleight of Hand +10 (Expertise)▶
Pickpocketing, planting items, concealing objects. Also used for disabling non-magical traps. Passive is 20 — most things just happen without a roll.
Thieves' Tools +6▶
Pick locks and disarm magical traps (uses DEX + Proficiency = +6). Psychic Blades can't help here — bring the actual tools. Psi-Bolstered Knack can bail you out on a failed roll.
Sneak Attack — 3d6 extra damage, once per turn
Triggers: Attack with a Finesse or Ranged weapon while you have advantage on the attack, OR while a conscious ally is within 5 ft of the target (and you don't have disadvantage).
Cunning Strike: Spend 1d6 of Sneak Attack dice (reducing SA to 2d6) to add one effect. Save DC 15.
Poison — CON save or Poisoned 1 min Trip — DEX save or Prone Withdraw — move half speed after attack

Bonus Actions & Reactions
Cunning ActionBonus Action▶
Each turn, use your bonus action to Dash (double speed), Disengage (no OAs until end of turn), or Hide (Stealth check vs. passive perceptions).
Steady AimBonus Action▶
If you haven't moved this turn, use your bonus action to gain advantage on your next attack roll this turn. Your speed becomes 0 for the rest of the turn. Great for guaranteed Sneak Attack when no ally is nearby.
Psychic Blade (offhand)Bonus Action▶
After attacking with a Psychic Blade this turn, if your other hand is free, manifest a second blade as a bonus action. Deals 1d4 + DEX (+4) = 1d4+4 psychic damage. Attack bonus +7. No dual-weapon feat required.
Uncanny DodgeReaction▶
When an attacker you can see hits you, use your reaction to halve the attack's damage against you. Does not work on area damage or attacks from unseen attackers.
Key Advantages & Notes
Alert feat: Initiative = DEX +4 + Prof +3 = +7. After rolling, can swap Initiative with any willing ally.
Halfling Luck: Reroll any natural 1 on a d20 test (attack, save, or check).
Brave: Advantage on saves to avoid or end the Frightened condition.
Vex mastery: If you hit with a Vex weapon, the target has disadvantage on its next attack roll before your next turn. Psychic Blades always have Vex.
Halfling Nimbleness: Move through spaces of creatures at least one size larger without difficulty.
Cunning Strike save DC: 8 + DEX +4 + Prof +3 = DC 15

Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Frightened condition.

Luck

Feats
Alert
Add your Proficiency Bonus (+3) to your Initiative rolls. After you roll Initiative, you can swap your Initiative with that of one willing ally in the same combat.
Skill Expert
+1 DEX (now 18). Gained Investigation proficiency. Gained Expertise in Stealth (doubles proficiency bonus for Stealth checks).
Rogue Features
Expertise (Level 1)
Double proficiency bonus for Sleight of Hand and Persuasion (chosen at level 1). Stealth expertise added via Skill Expert feat at level 4.
Sneak Attack (Level 1) — 3d6
Once per turn, deal 3d6 extra damage when attacking with a Finesse or Ranged weapon and you have advantage, OR a conscious ally is within 5 ft of the target and you don't have disadvantage.
Thieves' Cant (Level 1)
You know Thieves' Cant — a secret mix of dialect, jargon, and code that lets you hide messages in conversations. You also know Goblin as an additional language.
Weapon Mastery (Level 1)
Apply the Mastery property of 2 weapon types you're proficient with. Can change selections after a Long Rest. Psychic Blades always have Vex (Soulknife feature, independent of this).
Cunning Action (Level 2)
Bonus action each turn: Dash, Disengage, or Hide.
Steady Aim (Level 3)
Bonus action: Gain advantage on your next attack roll this turn. Requirement: you haven't moved this turn. Cost: your speed becomes 0 for the rest of the turn.
Cunning Strike (Level 5)
When you deal Sneak Attack damage, spend 1d6 (reducing SA from 3d6 to 2d6) to apply one of: Poison (CON save DC 15 or Poisoned 1 min), Trip (DEX save DC 15 or Prone), or Withdraw (move up to half speed immediately after attack).
Uncanny Dodge (Level 5)
Reaction: When an attacker you can see hits you with an attack, halve the attack's damage against you. Once per turn.
Soulknife Subclass
Psionic Power (Level 3) +1 Short Rest
6 Psionic Energy Dice (d8). Regain 1 die on Short Rest, all on Long Rest.
Psi-Bolstered Knack
When you fail an ability check using a skill or tool you're proficient with, roll one Psionic Energy Die (d8) and add it to the check. The die is expended only if the check now succeeds.
Psychic Whispers — 1 free per Long Rest
Magic action: Choose up to 3 creatures (Proficiency Bonus) you can see. Roll a d8. For that many hours, those creatures can communicate telepathically with each other even if they don't share a language. First use per Long Rest is free; additional uses each cost 1 psi die.
Psychic Blades (Level 3)
Manifest blades of psychic energy as part of the Attack action — no material needed, can't be disarmed. Primary blade: 1d6 psychic + DEX (+4), Finesse, Thrown 60/120, Vex mastery. Bonus action offhand blade (other hand free): 1d4+4 psychic. Both vanish immediately after the attack.
