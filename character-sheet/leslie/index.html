Roleplay & Exploration Cheat Sheet
Always available
Stealth +7 (passive 17)At will▶
Your go-to. Passive Stealth of 17 means you're hard to spot even without rolling. Use Naturally Stealthy to hide behind ANY creature larger than you — that includes most party members and many NPCs. Don't wait to be told you can hide; ask for it.
Sleight of Hand +7At will▶
Pickpocketing, planting items, palming objects, cutting purses. You're exceptional at this. Also covers fine manipulation tasks — disabling devices, sleight-of-hand tricks, anything requiring nimble fingers. Ask your DM when in doubt.
Thieves' Tools (Investigation +3)At will▶
Pick locks and disarm traps. Roll Investigation to examine a lock or trap first — knowing what you're dealing with helps. You have TWO sets of thieves' tools so losing one isn't fatal.
Cunning ActionBonus Action▶
Every turn you can Dash, Disengage, or Hide as a Bonus Action — no action cost. Disengage to escape melee without provoking opportunity attacks. Hide to set up Sneak Attack advantage. Dash to reposition fast.
Alert feat — initiative edge
Initiative +5 · swap with ally▶
You almost always go first. After rolling initiative, you can immediately swap your result with one willing ally who isn't Incapacitated — useful if a frontliner rolled low and needs to act before an enemy. Decide before anyone acts.

When do I qualify?Action▶
You qualify if EITHER condition is true:

① You have Advantage on the attack roll (e.g. from Vex, from ally flanking, after hiding), OR

② An ally is within 5ft of your target — that ally can't be Incapacitated, and you can't have Disadvantage on the roll.

Once per turn only, but does apply to Opportunity Attacks if you qualify.
Nick mastery (Dagger) — free extra attackFree▶
When you attack with a Light weapon and hit, Nick lets you make the extra Light weapon attack as part of the SAME Attack action — not a Bonus Action. This frees your Bonus Action for Cunning Action (Hide, Dash, Disengage).

Attack with Shortsword → if you want a second swing, use Dagger as your Light weapon and get the Nick attack free. One Nick attack per Attack action.
Vex mastery (Shortsword & Shortbow)Next turn▶
When you hit with a Vex weapon, you have Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn.

Track your Vex target in the box at the top of the page. That Advantage qualifies you for Sneak Attack automatically — no ally nearby needed.
Best combat openerCombo▶
Round 1: BA Hide (Cunning Action) → Attack with Shortsword with Advantage (from hiding) → Sneak Attack triggers → Vex now active on that target.

Round 2: Attack Vex target with Advantage → Sneak Attack again → consider Nick (Dagger) for a free second swing if your first attack hits.

Keep repeating: Vex keeps refreshing your Advantage each time you hit.
Key Advantages & Notes
Halfling Lucky: Any time you roll a 1 on a d20 test (attack, check, save) — reroll it, must use the new result
Brave: Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end Frightened
Naturally Stealthy: Can Hide even when only obscured by a creature one size larger than you
No reaction ability yet: Uncanny Dodge comes at level 5 — for now, just opportunity attacks
Halfling Traits
Brave
Advantage on saving throws you make to avoid or end the Frightened condition.
Halfling Nimbleness
You can move through the space of any creature that is a size larger than you, but you can't stop in the same space.
Luck
When you roll a 1 on the d20 of a D20 Test, you can reroll the die, and you must use the new roll.
Naturally Stealthy
You can take the Hide action even when you are obscured only by a creature that is at least one size larger than you.
Feats
Alert
+2 to Initiative rolls. Immediately after you roll Initiative, you can swap your Initiative with the Initiative of one willing ally in the same combat. You can't make this swap if you or the ally has the Incapacitated condition.
Rogue Features
Expertise — Sleight of Hand & Stealth
Your proficiency bonus is doubled for these two skills (+7 each).
Sneak Attack — 1d6
Once per turn, deal extra 1d6 damage when you have Advantage on the attack, OR an ally is within 5ft of the target (and you don't have Disadvantage). Scales with level.
Thieves' Cant
You know Thieves' Cant and one other language of your choice. A secret mix of slang, jargon, and code that rogues use.
Weapon Mastery — Nick & Vex
Nick (Dagger): The extra Light weapon attack can be made as part of the Attack action instead of a Bonus Action, once per turn. Vex (Shortsword, Shortbow): Hitting a creature gives Advantage on your next attack against it before end of your next turn.
Cunning Action
Bonus Action: Dash, Disengage, or Hide. Frees your main action for attacks while still repositioning or hiding every turn.
Coming at Level 3 — Soulknife
Psionic Energy Dice, Psychic Blades, Psi-Bolstered Knack, Psychic Whispers.
