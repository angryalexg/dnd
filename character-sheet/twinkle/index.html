Exploration & Utility
Investigation +5 / Arcana +5At will▶
Your sharpest tools. Investigation uncovers hidden things and solves puzzles. Arcana covers magical phenomena, creatures, and lore. Passive Investigation of 15 means you notice a lot without rolling.
Identify (Ritual) — no slotRitual▶
1 minute (11 min as ritual), Touch range. Instantly learn all properties of a magic item you're touching — no attunement needed just to identify it. Cast as ritual to save your spell slots.
Detect Magic (Ritual) — no slotRitual▶
10 min ritual, 30ft sphere, Conc 10 min. Detect the presence of magic within 30ft — you see auras around magical creatures or objects and can sense the school of magic. Great for scouting before entering a room.
Thieves' Tools / Forgery KitAt will▶
Thieves' Tools: pick locks and disarm traps using Dexterity. Forgery Kit: create convincing copies of documents — great for counterfeiting papers, forging signatures, or making fake passes. Both covered by Criminal background.
Tinker's Magic — create item3/Long Rest▶
As a Magic action while holding Tinker's Tools, create one tiny item from a provided list within 5ft. The item lasts until Long Rest. You have 3 uses. Also permanently knows the Mending cantrip.
Alert Feat
Initiative +3 · swap with ally▶
You add +2 to Initiative from Alert (total +3 with DEX). After rolling Initiative, you can immediately swap your result with one willing, non-Incapacitated ally. Very useful to give a frontliner a higher slot so they act before an enemy charges you.

Combat Spells — DC 13 · +5 to hit
Faerie Fire — best opening moveAction · 1st slot▶
60 ft range, 20 ft cube. DEX save DC 13 or all creatures in the area are outlined in light. Outlined creatures can't benefit from invisibility, and ALL attack rolls against them have Advantage — including your allies. Concentration up to 1 minute.

Why it's powerful: Every melee attacker in your party gains advantage on all their attacks vs those creatures. That's more net damage than any spell you could cast on your own. Cast this on a cluster of enemies and let your allies go to town.
Tasha's Caustic Brew — sustained acidAction · 1st slot▶
Self/30 ft Line. DEX save DC 13. On fail, targets are coated in acid. At start of each of their turns: 2d4 acid damage, save to end. Concentration up to 1 minute.

Best use: Against a slow, melee enemy who can't easily escape the ongoing damage. The damage adds up fast if they keep failing saves.
Catapult — solid damage, no V componentAction · 1st slot▶
60 ft range, S component only. DEX save DC 13. A 1–5 lb object flies at a target for 3d8 bludgeoning damage.

Note: No verbal component means you can use this even if you can't speak. Also works well with War Caster (reaction attacks). Grab an object from the environment — a rock, a tool, etc.
Grease — area controlAction · 1st slot▶
60 ft range, 10 ft square. DEX save DC 13 or Prone. The area is Difficult Terrain for the duration. Creatures who enter or start their turn there must also save or go Prone. Not concentration! Stack with Faerie Fire from an ally.

Best use: Doorways, chokepoints, to keep enemies from reaching you or your allies.
Absorb Elements — reactionReaction · 1st slot▶
Triggered when you take Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder damage. You gain Resistance to the triggering damage type until the start of your next turn — halving what you just took. Your next melee attack also deals +1d6 of that damage type.

No concentration. Very efficient emergency slot use.
War Caster — Reaction OA
Opportunity Attack = cast a spellReaction▶
When a creature leaves your reach without Disengaging, you can use your reaction to cast a spell targeting ONLY that creature, as long as it has a casting time of 1 Action.

Best options: Catapult (3d8, no V), Tasha's Caustic Brew (ongoing acid). Save Faerie Fire for Action since it needs to catch multiple targets.

Also: War Caster lets you perform somatic components even with weapons/shield in your hands.
